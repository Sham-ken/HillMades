Our Story

The Queen of Hill Stations

From the mist-shrouded peaks of the Nilgiri Mountains, we preserve the authentic recipes of woodfired varkey, pure cocoa chocolates, and estate teas.

Centuries-Old Tradition

The Legendary Ooty Varkey

Varkey was created over a century ago in the colonial woodfired bakeries of Ooty. Unlike ordinary puff pastries, real Ooty Varkey relies on the unique cool, dry mountain climate and wood-fired stone ovens to achieve its legendary layered crispiness.

We adhere strictly to the traditional woodfired baking techniques: unadulterated pure butter, natural yeast fermentation, and slow oven baking that gives our Varkey its golden, crumbly, melt-in-mouth texture.

Pure Cocoa Craft

Artisanal Homemade Chocolates

No visit to Ooty is complete without its famous homemade chocolates. Made with 100% pure cocoa butter, rich mountain milk, and freshly roasted cashews, almonds, and pistachios.

We don't use artificial stabilizers or cheap vegetable oils. Every bar and truffle is hand-tempered and poured to guarantee that signature velvety snap.
