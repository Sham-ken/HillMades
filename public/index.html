Our Roots Since 1982

A Century of Hill Station Baking Craft

Perched at 7,000 feet above sea level, the misty hill town of Ooty has perfected the art of slow-baking layered crispy Varkey in woodfired hearths and hand-tempering cacao into artisanal chocolates.

Every single pack is prepared in small, traditional batches using unadulterated pure butter, estate spices, and high-grown Nilgiri tea leaves. We seal each batch in airtight barrier pouches so you experience the true mountain aroma in every bite.
